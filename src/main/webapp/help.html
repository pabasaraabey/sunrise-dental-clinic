How to use this system

A short guide for staff who are new to the clinic.

Booking an appointment

1

Open New appointment

Choose New appointment from the menu on the left.

2

Enter the patient's details

Type the contact number carefully — it is how the system recognises returning patients. If the number is already registered, the existing record is reused and no duplicate is created.

3

Choose a dentist, treatment and date

The system shows each dentist's working hours and the cost of the selected treatment.

4

Pick a free slot

Free slots are selectable. Slots already booked appear greyed out and struck through, so you can see the whole shape of the dentist's day at a glance.

5

Book

The system generates the appointment number and shows it on screen. Write it on the patient's card — it is how the visit is found later.

Producing a bill

1

Open Billing and enter the appointment number

The visit details appear so you can confirm you have the right patient before charging anything.

2

Generate the bill

The consultation fee is added automatically and VAT is calculated for you. Patients aged 65 or over receive the senior concession without you needing to apply it.

3

Print the receipt

Opens as a PDF in a new tab, ready to print. Each visit can be billed only once.

Questions staff often ask

?

The slot I wanted turned grey while I was typing

Another receptionist booked it first. The system prevents two patients being given the same slot, so choose another time and book again.

?

I cannot cancel an appointment

Cancellation closes two hours before the appointment, so the clinic has a chance to offer the slot to someone else. Speak to the administrator if it needs cancelling anyway.

?

My account is locked

Three wrong passwords in a row lock an account for fifteen minutes. Wait, then try again.

?

A menu option is missing

You only see what your role allows. Receptionists handle bookings and billing; dentists see schedules and patient history; administrators manage staff, prices and reports.
